## Themes, and where GoPinKaro stands

| What users say | Status in GoPinKaro |
|---|---|
| Per-account pricing gets expensive with several accounts | Flat plans. Pro covers 3 accounts, Business 10. |
| Extra "credits" or add-ons push the real price above the sticker price | No credits. Separate AI quotas exist but are shown on the pricing cards. |
| Free tier is too small to judge the tool | Free is 50 pins a month, up from 30. |
| Published prices and limits are inconsistent or hard to find | One plan table drives the site, the API and the tests, so they cannot drift. |
| Mistakes at scale, such as 200 pins landing on one day | Pacing warning above 15 pins a day and for repeated images. It warns only and does not block. |
| Bulk upload saves hours | Bulk and CSV scheduling, page and sitemap import. |
| Support that answers small questions | Not measured. In-app help is limited today. |
| Agencies cannot send branded reports or collaborate well | Not built: no team seats, approvals or reports. |
| Only Pinterest, no other networks | Out of scope by design. |

## Where to push next, in order
1. **Autopilot completeness.** Auto-draft from new posts is the headline saving of time. v1 handles RSS. Sitemap and WordPress detection are next.
2. **In-app help and first-pin tutorial.** The one theme with no answer yet, and it matches the five-minute onboarding goal.
3. **Agency reports and approvals.** Only worth building once there are agency customers asking for it.

Open question for a follow-up: ask real users. A short survey or a few calls will be more reliable than review blogs, and Reddit can be read by a person even though the tool cannot.
