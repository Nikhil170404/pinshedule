## What this means
- The whole niche is small. The head term is about 480 searches a month, so rankings alone will not fill a business. Free tools, guides and AI-answer visibility matter as much as these pages.
- High CPC on the head terms (about $6) shows buyers are searching, but the low competition scores (0.02 to 0.14) mean organic pages can realistically rank.
- "Pinterest keyword tool" is the second-biggest term and our dashboard Keywords page is behind a login. A public, free keyword page could capture it. That needs verified data (Pinterest Trends via the user's connected account) and cannot show invented volumes.
- "Pinterest bulk scheduler" is tiny at 20. Keep its page but do not expand it first.
